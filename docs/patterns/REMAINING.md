# Patterns still to build, and why

The owner asked on 1 November 2026 not to lose track of any pattern. This page lists what is left, so nothing is forgotten. The library has 240 tutorials.

## Tiling Search's 45° list

The list is finished. P042, CM045 and H153, the last three from the Alhambra, were built from Wichmann's drawings on 1 November, and no photo confirms them yet. Seven tilings are left out on purpose: DW10A and SM4 are a library design with a second pattern over it, and F15, P17, P067, IRAN15 and IRAN4 are designs already in the library.

## Parked in the queue, waiting for a source

- **CM026, Generalife ceiling.** Built, but Wade's photo has frames the drawing lacks.
- **Córdoba Synagogue, second Ibn Tulun grille, Alhambra interlace and rosette.** See [the queue](QUEUE.md).
- **Darb-e Imam spandrel,** and the rest of [the 24 Upcoming patterns](#/upcoming).

## Tried on Tiling Search's other lists, not built

- **Lengths no crossing gives:** EGY1019 (Qaytbay), AJL7 (Aleppo).
- **Over 1000 pieces:** N13, S32A, SA1, N3, ZEL1, SM10, SM11, SM30, TOP4, RIG3, TUR1, MIR4, P190, MIR2, IRA0601, D3, STAR.
- **Not one repeating pattern:** TL1, SIVAS3, SIVAS4, BON15, MIR11, SM31, and Codex's eight two-level drawings (AS10, BR9, IND1016, SM1, IND0408, IND0917, SM12, ASL20).

## Tiling Search's other lists, not yet assessed

These were never looked at as candidates, and they are not promised:

- About 40 finer-angle tilings (7.5° and below), with stars of many points; some may not be constructible.
- 28 with only approximate geometry on Wichmann's page.
- 25 from the other lists whose angles are 45°, and 10 at 90°.
- The mathematical lists: regular polygons (D and T codes), two-uniform tilings (44), tilings not constructible by ruler and compass (92, which cannot be taught here as they stand), modern designs in Islamic style (22), Koran illuminations (24), square Kufic (23).

# Twelve-pointed stars

**Sources.** Envato Tuts+'s "Geometric Design: Working With 6 and 12" builds this pattern on the seven-circle grid: a six-pointed star in each circle, then a second one turned 30°, inking both outlines. No second step-by-step source and no photo of a real example were found; searches turned up twelve-pointed stars in Ibn Tulun and Mamluk grilles, but drawn differently.

**Steps.** The six-fold foundation and first star are shared with the snowflake; the second star joins every other point from the six circles. Measuring the source's drawings gave a star radius of exactly half the spacing between circles, so neighbouring stars touch point to point, and an overlay of our seven stars on the source's final drawing is exact. The tile is the hexagon touching the circle at the first star's points.

**Why abandoned.** The pattern matches its source, but without a photo of a real example it cannot be verified, so it is kept in `docs/patterns/abandoned/` (it passes every golden check) rather than shipped.

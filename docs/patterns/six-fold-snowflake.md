# Six-fold snowflake

**Sources.** Mamoot's "Six-Fold Snowflake" tutorial (mamootdiy.com, 2022) gives eight numbered steps with one drawing each, starting from the six-fold foundation in Mamoot's "Six-Fold Model". The same page shows the pattern in the Eski Mosque, the Mosque of Ibn Tulun and Shah-i-Zinda. A second step-by-step source for exactly this sequence was not found in about five searches; Charlotte Webb's ATM handout (slides 12–13) builds the same base of two triangles in a hexagon and carries lines out to the hexagon, but through the star's inner corners, which gives the sibling "star ringed by six hexagons" pattern rather than this one.

**Steps.** Steps 1–3 (line, mother circle, the six ring circles) matched the source's step 1 on the first try. Steps 4–6 (mirror lines through the large outer petals, the dynamic hexagon, the star of two triangles) matched the source's steps 2–4 on the first try. The first version of the small star drew it as two triangles and then the six lines again; the source draws only the six lines, so the triangles were dropped and the small star's inner corners found as crossings of pairs of lines, which matched source step 6 on the second try. The ink first looked different from the source's final trace because our render was zoomed out to fit the cell, but a close crop of the source showed every inked segment is the same: the outline of the large star, the outline of the small star, and each small-star line carried from its point out to the hexagon.

**Decisions.** The tile is the hexagon itself, because that is how the source repeats it and because free inking only offers construction inside the cell. A first version used the 60° rhombus joining O to two neighbouring centres, which cut the right pieces but failed the free-inking test, since the rhombus reaches into neighbouring hexagons where nothing is constructed. That led to hexagonal cells in the format. All points are picked by angle from O or as nearest to a named point, with margins of at least 30°.

**Result.** Five piece types: the small star (12 corners), hexagons centred on the corners of the tile (three per tile), chevrons, rhombi and triangles. The wall matches the Ibn Tulun window grille and the Eski Mosque panel: each small star is ringed by six hexagons, with the bands of chevrons, rhombi and triangles between them.

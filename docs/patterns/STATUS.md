Each pattern's log is in `docs/patterns/<id>.md`, and the overnight run is summarised in `docs/OVERNIGHT.md`.

## Existing tutorials that need your review

These two were in `tutorials/` before the run. They pass every check, but they could not be verified against a photo, and abandoning them is your call rather than mine.

- **eight-fold-zellige**: no photo of this exact rosette (eight-pointed star, eight petals, octagon in a star at the corners) was found on Wikimedia Commons or in Sandy Kurt's free zellige PDFs, which have no photos. `docs/geometry-notes.md` already says its steps 11–16 were reconstructed from text and may be wrong.
- **sixteen-fold-rosette**: compared with the Bou Inania zellige wall (Fès), the real sixteen-fold rosettes are much richer: the ground between them is filled with further rings, stars and octagons, where ours is one large ground piece. The rosette itself is plausible; the pattern around it is not yet the real one.

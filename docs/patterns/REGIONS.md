# Proposed region and tradition of every tutorial

Step 1 of [the library browsing plan](../LIBRARY-BROWSING-PLAN.md), proposed on 27 October 2026 by `tools/review/propose_regions.py` and checked by hand. The owner approved the list and both proposals on 27 October 2026, and `propose_regions.py --write` wrote it into the tutorials. Each tutorial gets one region, from the place it names, and one tradition, from its old free-text style cut down to a fixed list; where the style was vague ("Islamic", "Islamic tilework"), the region decides. Rows the rules got wrong were corrected by hand and say why.

## Two questions

1. **Patterns with no building.** The classic constructions (six-fold snowflake, eight-fold star and cross, Samira Mian's rosettes and others) sit under Books and other, and keep the tradition their author gave them (Mamluk, Moroccan…). The alternative is to call them all Islamic. Keeping them is the proposal, because each was chosen for a tradition it belongs to.
2. **Moroccan or Marinid.** Marinid is used for the Marinid buildings (Bou Inania, Attarine, Chellah, Salé), and Moroccan for the rest of Morocco, including Saadian and Alaouite work. The alternative is Moroccan for all of Morocco, which is simpler but loses the dynasty.

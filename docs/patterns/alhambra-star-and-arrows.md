# Alhambra star and arrows

**Sources.** Mamoot's "Rosette Tile from the Alhambra" gives eight steps with a drawing each, built on its four-fold model, and a photo of the rosette on a painted ceiling at the Alhambra. An earlier attempt also saved a Spanish page on the "rueda de lazo" and a Grid Maker Pro page, which were not needed for the rosette.

**Steps.** The opening is the classic two squares from the star-and-cross tutorial. The small circle (radius from a corner of the standing square to the nearest crossing of the squares, √2 − 1), its eight points, and the star joining every third point matched the source's steps 4–6 on the first try. The ink was found by sampling the source's final drawing along every construction segment, and an overlay of our ink on that drawing shows every line in place.

**Why abandoned.** The rosette is right, but the pattern needs a tile, and no photo or source shows this rosette repeated. The source itself says it "doesn't produce a very interesting tessellation" and shows it only alone, and the Alhambra photo is a single rosette inside a larger network of bands that could not be reconstructed from that one picture. Repeating it on a plain square grid gives rosettes touching tip to tip with a large ground between, which is our own invention, so it cannot be verified against a real wall. The finished file, which passes every golden check with that square repeat, is kept in `docs/patterns/abandoned/` rather than `tutorials/`, so it does not ship. It could return as a tutorial if the band network around the ceiling rosette is worked out, or if the format gains single-motif tutorials without a repeat.

Status: abandoned: rosette verified step by step, but no real repeating pattern of it was found, so the square repeat would be invented

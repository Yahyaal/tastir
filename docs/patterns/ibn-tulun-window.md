# Ibn Tulun window

**Sources.** Mamoot's "A Window from Ibn Tulun Masjid", learned in a class by William Charles Riding, gives nine steps with a drawing each, two tilings, and Riding's photo of the window grille. An Aramco World tutorial of an Ibn Tulun window turned out to be a different window, so there is only one step-by-step source.

**Steps.** The twelve divisions, the hexagon and two triangles, the three squares and the twelve-pointed star (each point joined to the point five places round) follow the source's steps 1–4. The source's "anglefish" and its two stars that "bend" at the diagonals are not lines through two marked points in the text, so they were measured: a crop of step 5 showed the fish's fins run along the square sides from the nose to the triangle, and its tail is a cross of two lines from where the nose's square meets the hexagon, through the point on the axis where the other two squares cross. The small star then runs along the twelve-pointed star's lines and the large star along lines through the fin tips parallel to them, the two families crossing on the diagonals. Checked against the final drawing with the segment-sampling method, every black segment is one of these, and the overlay of our ink on the source's final drawing is exact. The colours in the source are squares green and star purple, which an earlier reading had swapped.

**Decisions.** The tile is the hexagon through the six noses, which gives the source's "clover" tiling, where three fish meet nose to nose. Its lattice runs the same way as the grille's (neighbours straight up and at ±30°).

**Result.** Five piece types: the central star, three-armed openings where three motifs meet, chevrons, small heart-shaped pieces, and the small squares where the two stars cross. The wall matches the source's clover tiling, and Riding's photo shows the same openings: stars ringed by chevrons, three-armed openings between motifs, and small square holes. The photo is small, so the match is by those features rather than by measurement.

Status: verified

# Four-fold ring of circles

**Sources.** Sandy Kurt's free "4fold Pattern Tutorial" gives twelve steps with a drawing each: a circle divided into eight, the two squares and the tile square, eight small circles on the squares' crossings touching the axes and diagonals (the ring described in `docs/geometry-notes.md` for the Fès panel), a ninth at the centre and quarter circles at the corners, then a lying and a standing square inside every small circle with their sides carried across the tile. An earlier attempt also gathered Samira Mian's and the Linlithgow grid worksheets, the ATM handout, and photos of a Mughal jali at Sikandra and of the al-Hakim mosque.

**Steps.** The construction is our own grouping of the same elements: every long line of the final pattern is a side of one small circle's square, so the tutorial builds each of the 40 needed lines once, from the circle that defines it. The ink was found by sampling Kurt's final drawing along every candidate line (99% of its black pixels are covered; the rest are stroke joints), and an overlay of our ink on the drawing is exact. The file was generated by `docs/patterns/abandoned/gen-four-fold-ring-of-circles.py` and passes every golden check.

**Why abandoned.** Kurt does not name the monument, and no photo of this exact pattern was found. The Sikandra jali that looked similar is a different pattern: its large eight-pointed stars sit among smaller octagons and plain crossings, while this pattern has ten equal stars per tile. A pattern that cannot be checked against a real example cannot be marked verified. Kurt's PDF is also marked all rights reserved, and our construction, though grouped differently, follows her sequence closely, which is worth your judgement before shipping even with credit. The finished file is kept in `docs/patterns/abandoned/`.

Status: abandoned: matches its only source exactly, but no photo of a real example was found, and the source is all-rights-reserved

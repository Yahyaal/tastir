# Free Photo Research for Alhambra Patterns

Research conducted 9 October 2026 for free Creative Commons or public domain photos of Alhambra patterns currently using private photos.

## Search Methodology

Searched Wikimedia Commons and Flickr for Creative Commons licensed photos of specific Alhambra geometric patterns. Each pattern was compared visually with the app's wall view to determine match quality.

## Challenges Encountered

1. **Specific vs. General**: Many Wikimedia Commons photos show the general room or architectural features rather than close-ups of specific geometric tile panels.

2. **Documentation Gaps**: Photos are often categorized by room name (Sala de los Reyes, Comares bath) but not by the specific geometric pattern shown, making it difficult to identify which panel contains which design.

3. **Angle and Detail**: Tourist photos are often taken at angles or distances that don't capture the geometric pattern clearly enough to compare with tutorial diagrams.

4. **Pattern Repetition**: Nasrid geometric designs repeat across different rooms in similar but not identical configurations. A pattern "of the same type" is easier to find than "the exact panel."

## Recommendations

1. **alhambra-bath-stars**: No suitable free photo found. The bath photos show architecture, not tiles in detail. Consider contacting Wikimedia Commons photographers who have shot the Alhambra baths to request a detail shot.

2. **alhambra-barca-stars**: Similar situation - general room photos exist but not pattern details. A return photography trip specifically for geometric patterns would be valuable.

3. **alhambra-reyes-stars**: Best chance of finding a match by reviewing all 40+ images in the category systematically, looking for tile dadoes rather than ceiling paintings.

4. **alhambra-reyes-fret**: Good candidates exist in the Arabesque details category. Review for best geometric match.

5. **alhambra-comares-frames**: The Mexuar alternative is a strong candidate as it uses the identical geometric vocabulary.

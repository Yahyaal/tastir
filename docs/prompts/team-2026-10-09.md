## Prompt for Grok — an outsider's test, free photos, and the Arabic

You have your own computer and access to the GitHub repository `Yahyaal/tastir`. Clone it fresh. Tastir is a web app that teaches Islamic geometric patterns one compass or ruler step at a time; learners can watch a tutorial, draw along with it, and colour the result. You are **not** to change the app's code. Do three things.

### 1. Test the app as a newcomer

Install with `pnpm install` and run `pnpm dev` (http://localhost:4200). Use it as a hobbyist who has never seen it, in a desktop browser (1280×800 and 1440×900) and in a phone-sized window with touch. From the home page, by clicking only what you can see:
- watch two tutorials to the end and colour them;
- press **Draw along** on "Comares bones" and on "Eight-fold star and cross": draw every step yourself, ink with the pen, repeat the tile yourself, colour it and keep it; reload once in the middle;
- make a **New pattern** in the editor (a circle, some lines, a tile, ink, play);
- open the **Upcoming patterns** page.

For each problem, open a GitHub issue: a short title, what you did, what you expected, what happened, a screenshot, and the window size. Label it `bug` (something broken, or anything that moves, overlaps or is cut off) or `suggestion` (something confusing). Then open one summary issue, "Outsider test, 9 October", listing all of them most important first, with your verdict on whether a beginner could use the app alone.

### 2. Find free photos

Several patterns are compared with photos we are not allowed to publish. Search Wikimedia Commons (and Flickr with Creative Commons licences) for a **free, straight-on, sharp photo of the same panel** for each of these tutorials (open the tutorial in the app, press "Result and original", and compare the wall with your candidate):
- `alhambra-bath-stars`, `alhambra-barca-stars`, `alhambra-reyes-stars`, `alhambra-reyes-fret`, `alhambra-comares-frames` (Alhambra, Granada);
- and for the patterns on the Upcoming page whose photo says "shown on the dev server only".

Put your results in a new file `docs/research-candidates.md` on a branch `grok/free-photos`, as a table: tutorial or pattern, the photo's page URL, its licence, its creator, its size, how well it matches (exact panel, same design elsewhere, or no match) and a one-line note. Do not add the image files. Open a pull request.

### 3. Check the Arabic

`docs/prompts/arabic-titles.md` lists the Arabic subtitles shown on the pattern cards, with their English titles. For each one, say whether it is correct, natural Arabic for that title, and suggest a better one where needed, with a short reason. Add your answers to the same pull request, as a new file `docs/arabic-titles-review.md`.

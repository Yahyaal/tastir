## Why

Tastir teaches Islamic geometric patterns one ruler-and-compass step at a time. Since 25 October more than a hundred tilings from Brian Wichmann's [Tiling Search list of 45° tilings](https://tilingsearch.mit.edu/sim/sim6.htm) have been added as tutorials. Fourteen could not be, because the tutorial format requires every pattern to turn into itself by at least a third of a turn about O (`fold` ≥ 3). These fourteen turn only by a half (wallpaper groups pmm and cmm), or not at all, with only a mirror (pm). The task is to let the format, the engine and the player handle `fold: 2` and `fold: 1`, and then build the fourteen.

## Part 1: support fold 2 and fold 1

`fold` is how many times the pattern turns into itself about O in a full turn. Today it is `z.int().min(3)` in `packages/schema/src/lib/tutorial.ts`. These are the places that assume three or more; check each, and search for others (`fold`, `rot3`, `hasSymmetry`, `imagesOf`):

1. **Schema** (`packages/schema/src/lib/tutorial.ts`): allow `fold` 1 and 2, and add `rot2` to the repeat transforms (`rot3` … `rot16` and `mirror:<line>` exist today). `star` stays ≥ 3, because a star always has at least three points. Update `docs/tutorial-format.md` to say what fold 1 and fold 2 mean and when to use them: fold 2 for pmm, cmm and p2-type tilings with a half-turn point at O, and fold 1 for a tiling with only mirrors or glides, where O sits on a mirror line.
2. **Engine** (`packages/engine/src/lib/checks.ts`, `hasSymmetry`, and the golden test `packages/engine/src/golden.spec.ts`): the golden test checks the pieces near O for `fold`-fold symmetry. With fold 2 it must check the half-turn, and the mirror unless `chiral`. With fold 1 the rotation check is trivial, so it must check the mirror about the line through O and A instead. A pattern whose O is badly placed must still fail. Add unit tests for both cases, including one that fails as it should.
3. **The cell and the repeat.** Rectangular cells already work: `alhambra-arrayanes-portico-doors` (P077) is pmm, and was built with `fold: 4` only because OA was chosen so that the pieces near O happen to be four-fold. Make sure a rectangular or rhombic (cmm) cell repeats correctly with fold 1 and 2, and add an engine test with a small pmm and a small cmm tutorial fixture.
4. **Project core and player.** Check everything that uses `fold`:
   - free inking copies an inked segment by its `fold` turns and mirrors (`imagesOf` in `packages/project-core/src/finish.ts`, used by `apps/player/src/along/useInkBoard.ts` and `apps/player/src/editor/Editor.tsx`);
   - the editor's fold input (`Editor.tsx`, `AboutPanel.tsx`);
   - the colour stage's rosettes and rings (`apps/player/src/app/rings.ts`, `rosettes.ts`), which may group pieces in rings by turning about O;
   - the library's fold filter and labels (`Library.tsx`, `browsing.ts`, `build/catalog.tsx`, `PrintSheet.tsx`, `PatternNav.tsx`): a fold-1 pattern should read sensibly, for example "mirror" rather than "1-fold";
   - draw along (`apps/player/src/along/`).

   Each must work, or degrade sensibly, for fold 1 and 2. Add tests where behaviour changes.
5. **No change for existing tutorials.** Every existing tutorial must give exactly the same pieces, ink and repeat as before. The golden tests prove that.

Commit Part 1 as its own commits, in the style of the surrounding code, before starting Part 2. Run the full checks after Part 1 (see below).

## Part 2: build the fourteen

Use the builders' method from `docs/prompts/tilingsearch-45-builders-2026-10-25.md`:
1. Read the PDF into pieces.
2. Snap the corners to exact values: a + b·√2/2, or (p + q√2)/7, thirds, sixths, or a star radius such as 1/(3 − √2).
3. Devise a ruler-and-compass construction from O and A; tutorials never contain coordinates.
4. Write the tutorial with a generator in `tools/review/tilingsearch_45/`, and format it with `python3 tools/review/format_tutorial.py`.
5. Check the piece areas by hand against the cell area.
6. Overlay our ink on the PDF and look at it.

Place O at a half-turn point (fold 2) or on a mirror line (fold 1), at the centre of the most important motif, and A so that OA is a natural unit. The helpers in `tools/review/tilingsearch_45/` and `tools/review/tilingsearch_45/large/` (see its README) do most of the reading.

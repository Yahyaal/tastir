You are working on Tastir (GitHub repo `Yahyaal/tastir`), a web app that teaches Islamic geometric patterns one compass or ruler step at a time. Make a branch `app/ink-speed-2` from the latest `main`, run `pnpm install`, and read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/PROJECT.md` first.

## Where it stands

When a learner presses Ink in draw along, the pen should work within one second. Commit `3138258` ("Ink speed 3") got the click from 9.6 s to 2.9 s on `bou-inania-rosette` and from 13.5 s to 3.4 s on `bou-inania-twelve-fold-wall`; `kurt-eight-fold-zellige-shifted` is already under a second. The second is about 1 s of the browser painting the board, with 118,000 or 191,000 dashed bits and 60,000 or 96,000 pen dots. `pnpm bench:ink` and `apps/player/e2e/ink-speed.spec.ts` measure it.
